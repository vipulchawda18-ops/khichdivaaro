Our Story

Born from a
grandmother's recipe

At Desi Khichdivaaro, we believe khichdi is more than just food — it's a feeling. It's the taste of home, of care, of simplicity at its finest. We started with a passion to bring authentic, nourishing khichdi to the heart of Bhilai-Durg.

Every variety we serve is crafted fresh daily using quality ingredients and time-honoured recipes. From classic moong dal to spiced masala and seasonal specials — there's a bowl for every mood.

Freshly prepared every day
